# NEXUS Desktop — The Room

**Version 1.0 · July 2026 · A new interaction paradigm. Rejects the page/dashboard model of
07–09 entirely. The Instrument tokens and honesty rules (01–02) survive; every screen,
sidebar, table, and card does not.**

---

## Part 1 — Three concepts, honestly compared

The test applied to all three: *a stranger watches 20 seconds and says "I've never seen
software work like that."* And the standing constraint: every behavior must be the real
backend — the invention is already in there; no concept may fake what it can't compute.

### Concept A — the living knowledge organism ("the reef")

Beliefs are organisms in a continuous space. Confidence is luminosity and mass; evidence is
nourishment; premise-links are tissue. Death is visible decay; the sediment layer below is
the fossil record. Contradictions circle each other. The space is never still — it breathes
at the rate of the record.

- **Memorable because:** nobody has seen knowledge *age and die* as an organism.
- **Iconic interaction:** press-and-hold a belief and watch everything that would die with
  it wilt — the cascade as ecology.
- **Keynote moment:** a time-lapse of three weeks of a real mind: growth, death, fossils.
- **Shared online as:** "my AI's beliefs are literally dying, look at this."
- **vs ChatGPT:** there is no conversation at all — you tend a population, not a thread.
  **vs Obsidian:** nodes are mortal and earn their place; nothing is filed. **vs Cursor:**
  nothing here is a file; the organism is the artifact.
- **Why not:** the metaphor decorates faster than it informs. Confidence-as-glow violates
  the foundation (truthful before beautiful) the moment a viewer asks "what exactly does
  brightness mean?" — the honest answer is a number the glow obscures. It demos gorgeously
  and reads as an AI aquarium by week two. Rejected as *primary* — its motion vocabulary
  (wilt, settle, sediment) is stolen for the winner.

### Concept B — the adaptive thinking workspace ("the room that thinks")

There is no navigation. There is one room and one line. The room is the mind's current
prose — every conviction is a floating *sentence* whose typographic weight IS its earned
confidence. The line accepts anything: a question reorganizes the room around it (relevant
convictions step forward and sharpen, irrelevant ones blur back — computed by the backend's
own `rank_bets`: *similarity proposes, calibration decides*); an observation is witnessed
and, unprompted, becomes reflection; a stated belief triggers the counter-question — **"What
would change my mind?"** — before the room will hold it; an instruction becomes a proposal
the room makes back to you, effects-first. Coming back after days opens with *"While you
were away —"*: what died, what survived, what can't both be true. All computed, never
composed.

- **Memorable because:** the workspace visibly *thinks with you* — ask it something and
  watch your own audited convictions rearrange themselves into the shape of the problem.
- **Iconic interaction:** typing "should I ship on friday?" and seeing the room reorganize
  — two heavyweight sentences step forward, one contradiction slides between them, and the
  mind says what it *doesn't* know.
- **Keynote moment:** the return. Three days away, one screen: "While you were away, one
  assumption died. Want to see why?"
- **Shared online as:** a 15-second clip captioned "I asked my AI a question and it didn't
  answer — it showed me what I already believe, ranked by how often I've been right."
- **vs ChatGPT:** a question doesn't produce prose, it produces *your own beliefs, arranged*
  — with the receipts. **vs Obsidian:** you never file, link, or organize anything; the
  organization is computed from merit and relevance. **vs Cursor:** the object being edited
  is not code but conviction — and the AI must ask permission through an effect list.
- **Risk:** the room must never feel random. Mitigation: every reorganization prints its
  cause in one small line ("heard as a question — 4 convictions bear on it").

### Concept C — the AI operating system for reasoning ("mission control for a mind")

Every unit of cognition — an appraisal, a plan, a critique, a verdict — is a live *process*
with an inspectable working set. The surface is a reasoning scheduler: processes spawn,
cite, contend, and terminate; the user is the kernel's final authority.

- **Memorable because:** you watch machine reasoning as processes with receipts.
- **Iconic interaction:** freezing a judgment mid-flight and opening "what the model saw."
- **Keynote:** "this is the first AI with a task manager for its own thoughts."
- **Shared online as:** screenshots of derivation traces.
- **vs ChatGPT/Obsidian/Cursor:** genuinely novel — but for operators, not thinkers.
- **Why not:** it is the most honest concept and the least human one. It inspects
  intelligence rather than collaborating with it — a *debugger* view. And structurally it
  tends back toward panels-of-processes: the dashboard reborn. Its soul (any conclusion
  can open into "because —") is absorbed into the winner as the evidence thread.

### The choice: **B**, with A's motion vocabulary and C's receipts

The room that thinks — where sentences carry their own weight (A's organism, disciplined
into typography: weight is a *number* you can read, not a glow), and any sentence opens
into its receipts (C's soul, without the mission-control chrome).

## Part 2 — The Room: paradigm specification

### The three surfaces (there are no others)

1. **The prose** — the mind's standing convictions as floating sentences. Typographic
   weight, size, and presence are the earned fold (mapped, not decorated: 0.50 prior reads
   as ordinary text; a many-times-survived conviction is heavy and large; a shaky one —
   premises died — is thin, pale, and marked *"shaky — a reason I believed this died"*).
   Below the prose, folded to one line, the sediment: *"N things I was wrong about —"*.
2. **The line** — one input, bottom center: *"Say anything — a question, something you saw,
   something you believe, something to do."* Everything enters here. The room always echoes
   how it heard you ("heard as a question") so reorganization never feels random.
3. **The voice** — one quiet sentence, top: the presence line, always computed
   ("two of these can't both be true" / "a proposal is waiting for your yes").

### The five moments (what the room does)

| You give it | It does | The moment |
|---|---|---|
| a question | `rank_bets` + `recall` reorganize the prose; bearing convictions step forward and sharpen, others blur; moments (episodes) surface as small quotes | *the room thinks with you* |
| a belief | it asks back: **"What would change my mind?"** — refusing to hold what can't lose (admission rule as dialogue) | *the machine has standards* |
| an observation | "Noted." — and after enough of them, unprompted: *"I've been thinking about what you told me —"* new convictions materialize citing your words; near-beliefs it refused itself are shown struck | *it was actually thinking* |
| an instruction | it works, then makes a proposal back: "I want to change these 2 files — here is exactly what. Yes or no?" A no asks *why* and becomes a worry it carries into similar work; a later yes retires that worry — visibly | *it fears, and gets over it honestly* |
| your return | "While you were away —" what died (and what killed it), what survived, what appeared, what can't both be true. Each line opens into *why* | *it has been alive* |

### The language layer (backend → human, total)

falsifier → **"I'd change my mind if —"** · confidence/fold → **"how sure"** (weight +
"survived ×4 · never wrong") · belief/prediction/premortem → **conviction / "I expect —" /
"I worry —"** · falsified → **"turned out wrong"** · expired → **"went stale"** · retracted
→ **"let go — its evidence was erased"** · unjustified → **"shaky"** · resolution note →
**"then: …"** · provenance → **"because — you said…"** · contradiction → **"these can't
both be true"** · effect list → **"exactly what I want to change"** · settlement → **"that
worry turned out unnecessary"** · consolidate → **reflect** · the record → **memory** ·
verify → **"every memory provable"**. Raw terms remain one layer down (the classic surface,
kept at `/classic` for operators) — never in the room.

### The motion vocabulary (epistemic verbs only, from A)

**step forward / sharpen** (bears on your question) · **blur back** (doesn't) · **gain
weight** (survived) · **thin** (shaky) · **fall + strike** (died — the sentence drops out
of the prose into the sediment) · **interpose** (contradiction slides in) · **materialize**
(born from reflection, staggered) · **retire** (a worry settles, softly). Nothing loops,
nothing idles; stillness between changes is the proof the motion means something.

### The 20-second test, restated

A stranger watches you type *"should I ship on friday?"* — no menus touched — and sees the
room rearrange into two heavyweight sentences, one tension slid between them, and a line of
small quotes from your own past. That is not a dashboard, a chat, a vault, or an editor.
That is the reaction the paradigm is built to earn.

## Part 3 — Implementation notes

- Ships as the **default surface** of `nexus-desktop` (`/`); the prior instrument surface
  moves to `/classic`, untouched, for operators (providers, ledger, keynote tooling).
- One new bridge route: `GET /api/focus?q=` → `runtime::retrieval::rank_bets` +
  `recall_episodes` — the reorganization is backend-computed, not a frontend trick.
- "While you were away" is a pure diff of record events after the last-seen timestamp;
  if nothing happened, the room says so — the empty case is also honest.
- Every room sentence opens into receipts: I'd-change-my-mind-if, because (evidence with
  excerpts), how sure (the fold and its history), what rests on it.
- No sidebar, no tabs, no tables, no cards, no settings page. The room, the line, the voice.
